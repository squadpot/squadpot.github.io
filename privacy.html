SquadPot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

SquadPot (“the app”) helps friends and flatmates track and split shared expenses. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your data stays on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

1. What the app stores

  • The trips and homes you create, the names you type for the people in them, and their optional WhatsApp numbers and UPI IDs.
  • Expenses, payments, kitty / advance amounts, bills and daily logs you enter.

All of this is stored only on your device. We (the developer) never receive it, and it is not uploaded to any server.

2. What leaves your phone, and only when you ask

  • Currency rates: for foreign trips the app downloads exchange rates from exchangerate-api.com. The request contains no personal information (only which currencies to fetch); like any website, that service can see your IP address.
  • WhatsApp, UPI and sharing: when you tap “Request on WhatsApp”, “Pay via UPI”, “Share” or “Export CSV”, the app opens WhatsApp, your UPI app or your phone’s share sheet with the text, amount or file you chose. What happens next is governed by those apps’ own privacy policies.

3. What we do not do

  • No sign-up or login, so we hold no account data.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share any data.
  • The app never reads your SMS, contacts, location or bank details, and it never moves money: UPI payments happen inside your own UPI app.

4. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Play may collect a technical crash report (device model, Android version, the error) as part of its standard Android vitals, subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. These reports contain no expense data.

5. Deleting your data

Delete any trip or home from its settings screen, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

7. Your rights (India)

We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. As we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data of yours with us to access, correct or erase. If you have any question, contact us below.

8. Changes

If a future version adds features such as login or cloud sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
